Organization B - Northstar Services
Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of annual leave each calendar year and 10 working days of paid sick leave.

Employees request annual leave in the People Portal at least ten working days before the first leave day. Managers approve or decline requests in the portal.

Parental leave is available to full-time employees after 12 months of continuous employment. Requests require supporting documentation and must be submitted to HR.
