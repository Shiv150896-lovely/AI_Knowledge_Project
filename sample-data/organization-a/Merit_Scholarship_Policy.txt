Organization A - Acme University
Merit Scholarship Policy

Eligibility requirements

1. Applicants must have a GPA of 3.50 or higher.
2. Only students in year 2 or year 3 may apply.
3. Applicants with an existing sports scholarship are not eligible for the merit scholarship.
4. Applications are submitted through the Student Funding Portal before 30 June.

The merit scholarship supports academic achievement. The sports scholarship is a separate award and cannot be held with the merit scholarship.
